About WorkforceAI

AI-assisted autonomous decision workflow with human approval.

WorkforceAI is an explainable workforce optimization platform that helps organizations allocate tasks under changing availability, priority, workload, and SLA conditions.

It combines a transparent multi-factor scoring model with a global optimization layer and a scenario simulator that supports safe previewing before production changes are applied.

The system is designed for decision support rather than silent autonomous actions, and requires human review before final changes are persisted.
